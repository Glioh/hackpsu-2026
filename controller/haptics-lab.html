iPhone haptics experiments

Try two-tab hands-free haptics experiment

Try low-frequency pulses vs high-frequency audio

Keep System Haptics enabled. A switch changing or a log entry does not prove vibration: record what you physically feel. No sound is used.

1. Native control

This is the baseline. If it does not tick, check system settings before interpreting other results.

2. Scripted and delayed

After starting the delayed test, lift your finger and leave the screen alone. A tick at five seconds would be relevant to incoming hits.

Hold test runs once per press. A tick on release is different from a tick at the five-second trigger.

3. Native switch drag

Touch the switch and slowly slide left/right. First try ordinary dragging. Then arm the experiment and repeat: after five seconds, the switch will move relative to your finger during touch movement. This is experimental; it does not establish automatic haptics without touch movement.



Repeat in Safari and, optionally, after adding this page to the Home Screen. These tests do not join a room or change gameplay.
